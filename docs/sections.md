# Website sections

The landing page is one scrolling page. This doc lists every section, in order, with its job and content. **Update it whenever a section is added, removed, reordered or finished.**

## Who the site is for

| Audience | What they need | What we want them to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Recruiters (primary)** | Fast proof of senior level skill: work, experience, stack | **Contact Poshitha about a job** (email, LinkedIn, CV) |
| Anyone viewing the profile | Understand Poshitha professionally **and** as a person | Remember Poshitha beyond the CV |

**Principles for the flow**
- Proof comes early. Recruiters skim, so work and experience sit high on the page.
- Contact is always within reach (CV download and contact link), not only at the bottom.
- The personal side ("Beyond the code") comes after the proof and before contact, so it is the last impression.
- The visual theme ("Building my own legacy": discipline, long-term craft) runs through every section. See `website/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

## Section order

```
1. Hero             photo + headline           (owner redesigning)
2. About            who I am, how I work
3. Work             3–6 selected projects       ← main proof for recruiters
4. Experience       timeline of roles
5. Skills           grouped stack
6. Beyond the code  hobbies and activities      ← lifestyle side
7. Principles       poster style quotes / tagline moment
8. Contact          email · CV · links
   Footer
```

## Sections in detail

### 1. Hero
- **Job:** who Poshitha is and the mood of the site, in five seconds.
- **Content:** full screen photo (art directed desktop / mobile), headline in the signature composition (script word over display headline).
- **Status:** built. Multilingual welcome: a cursor types "Hello," and "I'm a senior full stack engineer based in Sri Lanka!", then the greeting cycles through Sinhala, Tamil and other languages. Section links sit on the wall at the top (transparent, scroll away with the hero); no scroll prompt; every section below is a placeholder until built. No header or CV button (owner removed it); CV download to come back in Contact. No contact button or side quotes (owner removed them). Facts come from the owner's career history. Don't change it without the owner.

### 2. About
- **Job:** the person behind the work: why Poshitha builds, how they think, what kind of engineer they are.
- **Content:** short story (3–5 sentences), current role and location, optional portrait.
- **Status:** not started. **Needs from owner:** bio, role, location.

### 3. Work (selected projects)
- **Job:** the strongest proof of senior level ability.
- **Content:** 3–6 projects. Each: name, the problem, Poshitha's role, stack, the outcome (measurable where possible), links (live, repo, case study).
- **Status:** not started. **Needs from owner:** project list and details.

### 4. Experience
- **Job:** show career progression and the companies behind it.
- **Content:** timeline of roles: company, title, dates, one or two results per role.
- **Status:** not started. **Needs from owner:** work history.

### 5. Skills
- **Job:** let a recruiter match Poshitha to a job spec at a glance.
- **Content:** stack grouped by area (frontend, backend, cloud / DevOps, data, tooling), not a wall of logos.
- **Status:** not started. **Needs from owner:** skill list.

### 6. Beyond the code
- **Job:** the lifestyle side: who Poshitha is outside development.
- **Content:** hobbies and activities, each with a short line and ideally a photo.
- **Status:** not started. **Needs from owner:** activities and photos.

### 7. Principles
- **Job:** give the page character and carry the poster theme. Also serves as the large tagline moment required by the design rules (words light up one by one on scroll).
- **Content:** poster style quotes and one big statement.
- **Status:** not started. **Needs from owner:** final wording.

### 8. Contact
- **Job:** turn interest into a message.
- **Content:** one clear call to action, email, LinkedIn, GitHub, CV download.
- **Status:** not started. **Needs from owner:** public email, links, CV file.

### Footer
- Name, links repeated, copyright. Privacy note if any analytics are added.
